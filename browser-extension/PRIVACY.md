# hustlen.ai Browser Extension – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

The hustlen.ai browser extension ("the extension") helps you apply to jobs. It autofills job application forms from your hustlen.ai profile and your saved answers, saves the jobs you apply to in your hustlen.ai account, and lets you tailor your CV and cover letter.

## What the extension accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Your hustlen.ai profile and CV.** Name, contact details, experience, education, skills and preferences. | To fill application forms. | Loaded from your hustlen.ai account. Kept in the browser's memory (`storage.session`) and cleared when the browser closes. |
| **Your saved answers.** Work authorization, salary expectations, notice period, custom Q&A, and the optional self-identification you choose to enter. | To fill recurring screening questions. | Stored **only in this browser** (`storage.local`). Never sent to hustlen.ai or anyone else. |
| **Answers you type in application forms.** Only if you turn on "Remember answers I type". | To offer saving them for next time. | Read only when you press Next or Submit. They stay in this browser and are saved only after you confirm. |
| **The job posting on the page you are viewing.** Title, company, location and description. | To save the job to your hustlen.ai applications and tailor your CV. | Sent to your hustlen.ai account **when you click "Save job", "Tailor" or "Autofill"**. |
| **Open screening questions.** The question text and its options. | To draft answers from your CV with AI, if you enable this. | Sent to hustlen.ai, which processes them to draft answers. No contact details are sent. |
| **Sign-in tokens.** | To connect the extension to your account. | Stored in the browser. You can revoke them at any time from your hustlen.ai dashboard or the extension. |

The extension **never collects** passwords, payment or bank details, government ID numbers, or your browsing history. It is **not active** on hustlen.ai itself, on local or development sites, or on sites that are clearly not job sites. You can also pause it on any site.

## What the extension never does

- It never submits an application for you. You review and submit every form yourself.
- It never sells your data or uses it for advertising. It never transfers your data to third parties, except the processing hustlen.ai needs to provide the features you use, such as AI drafting.
- It never allows humans to read your data, except when you ask for support, for security reasons, or when the law requires it.

The use of information received from the extension complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

- **Disconnect** from the extension, or from the hustlen.ai dashboard (*Browser extension → Disconnect*).
- **Turn off answer learning and AI drafting**, and delete saved answers, in *My answers*.
- **Remove the extension** from your browser. This deletes all locally stored data.
- To delete your hustlen.ai account data, contact privacy@hustlen.ai. Your hustlen.ai account is also governed by the hustlen.ai privacy policy at https://hustlen.ai/privacy.
